Privacy Policy

Codigix CRM · Effective 22 October 2026

This policy explains how Codigix Infotech Pvt. Ltd. ("Codigix", "we") collects, uses, stores and protects information in Codigix CRM (allinonecrm.codigixinfotech.com), a business platform for tasks, projects, sales and meetings that Codigix offers to businesses and their teams. It applies to every person who uses Codigix CRM.

Information we collect

Account information: name, work email, department, role and the password you sign in with (stored only as a secure hash).

Work records: the tasks, projects, meetings, notes, leads, quotations and other business records you and your team create in the platform.

Google account data, only if you choose "Connect my Google account", as described below.

Google account data and how we use it

Connecting Google is optional. When you connect, Google asks you to approve these permissions, and we use each one only for the purpose shown:

  • Your email address and basic profile (openid, email): to show which Google account is connected.
  • Google Calendar events (calendar.events): to create, update and cancel the calendar event for a meeting you schedule in Codigix CRM, with its Google Meet link and invitations to the attendees you choose. We do not read or change other events in your calendar.
  • Google Meet spaces created by this app (meetings.space.created): to add the meeting host as a co-host of a Meet that this app created.
  • Send email on your behalf (gmail.send): to send the emails you trigger in Codigix CRM (for example a task assignment, a quotation or a proposal) from your own Gmail address. We cannot read, search or delete the emails in your mailbox.

We store an encrypted Google access token (AES-256-GCM) so these actions work without asking you to sign in each time, and we store the calendar event ID and Meet link with the meeting record.

Codigix CRM's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features above. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and not read by people except with your permission, for security purposes, or where the law requires it.

How we share information

Records you create are visible to the members and managers of your own team's workspace who work with them, never to other customers. We do not sell personal data. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the platform (hosting, email delivery, and Google for the features you connect), or when the law requires it.

Storage, security and retention

Data is stored on secured servers. Access is limited to authorised staff, connections use HTTPS, and Google tokens are encrypted at rest. We keep work records for as long as your team uses the platform. A Google connection is kept until you disconnect it.

Your choices and removing Google access

  • Click Disconnect on the Meetings page: we revoke the Google token and delete it from our servers.
  • Or remove "Codigix CRM" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To access, correct or delete your personal data, email us at info@codigixinfotech.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use Google user data, we will update this page and ask for your consent again before using the data in a new way.
